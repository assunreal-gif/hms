Hostel Roles & Responsibilities

Designed with clear separation of duties for students and administration

👤 Resident / Student Role

Hostel Resident Portal

Students and residents can effortlessly report accumulated waste across hostel facilities.

  • ✓ Report waste in rooms, corridors, study halls, or mess
  • ✓ Categorize by Organic, Plastic, E-Waste, or Household
  • ✓ Set urgency priorities (Low, Medium, High, Urgent)
  • ✓ Receive unique Request ID for transparent tracking
Go to Waste Reporting →
🛡️ Hostel Admin Role

Superintendent / Sanitation Desk

Hostel wardens and maintenance supervisors oversee and assign collection tasks.

  • ✓ Centralized dashboard of all hostel waste requests
  • ✓ Inspect location, waste category, and urgency level
  • ✓ Update lifecycle: Pending → Assigned → Collected → Completed
  • ✓ Complete audit trail with timestamps and SQLite persistence

Accepted Waste Streams

Segregate waste at source to maintain a clean and hygienic campus environment

🍎

Organic Waste

Food leftovers, dining mess peelings, biodegradable pantry discards.

🥤

Plastic Waste

PET water bottles, food packaging wraps, wrappers, cartons.

💻

E-Waste

Broken chargers, cables, circuit components, batteries, keyboards.

📦

Household Waste

Room sweepings, paper, cartons, study stationery, miscellaneous.

🧱

Construction Waste

Tiles, wood fixtures, paint scraps, hostel repair debris.

♻️

Other Debris

Non-classified general debris, bulk packaging, seasonal items.
